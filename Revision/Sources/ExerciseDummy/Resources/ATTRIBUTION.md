# Simple Trainer V3 exercise human

The visible body, eyes, phenotype targets, joint helpers and initial game-engine skin weights derive from the MakeHuman Community's MPFB core art. The base mesh credits Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. The upstream art is released under CC0 1.0. [Pinned source](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/9de0707fdc88438d7c53a1a2ac7937d9221e2431/src/mpfb/data) · [License](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/9de0707fdc88438d7c53a1a2ac7937d9221e2431/LICENSE.md)

V3 fits this surface to the Exercise Markup Language humanoid, adds exercise-specific muscle contours and face groups, and exports normalized four-influence skinning and wrist twist data for Metal. The rig identifiers, pivot coordinates, grip bindings and contact landmarks follow the EML 1.1 reference profile. [EML project and profile credits](https://github.com/agrim/exercise-markup-language/tree/v1.1.0/anatomy)
