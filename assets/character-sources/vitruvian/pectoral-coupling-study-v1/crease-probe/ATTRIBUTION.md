# Pectoral crease attribution

The sharp superior/lateral nipple cleft is principally a donor-ownership problem in this prototype. It is already present before native CorrectiveSmooth, and remains when the three fan directions are replaced by one helper transform. The existing breast island moves away from adjacent skin that is mainly assigned to `DEF-chest_expand`.

At the two marked crease edges, the original fan yields native cage bends of 17.19° and 17.70°, versus 1.40° and 1.29° with the fan disabled. A single helper makes them sharper (29.47°/30.83°). A smoothly allocated, conserved combination of breast and chest_expand donors gives 2.35°/2.57°.

The original-fan attenuation control moves the measured chest patch by mean 24.619 mm; the compatible-donor allocation moves it by 24.604 mm (0.061% difference). Despite this near match, the attenuated original field retains 15.01°/15.44° bends and the visible sharp cleft. This separates the connected-surface improvement from merely reducing motion amplitude. These are descriptive local geometry measurements, not an anatomical scoring system.

Native full-stack gray images confirm the sharp superior cleft is greatly reduced. The broader curved inferior pectoral rim remains; no further neutral shape change was introduced. The source-authored rest candidate, posterior input guard, original native smoothing, three fan transforms per side, and all animation controls stay intact.

The new allocation conserves every vertex's total breast+chest_expand+helper influence. Its field is scaled by 0.671794908144298 to match the original aggregate transferred influence; this does not imply biological mass or tissue volume conservation.
